## Homepage artwork

The study scene is authored and maintained in [`artwork/study/room/study-master.aseprite`](artwork/study/room/study-master.aseprite) on a **640×360** base grid and exported at **3× (1920×1080)** to `public/art/study/study.png` and `public/art/study/props/`. The [furnished stone-room reference](artwork/study/references/reference.jpg) and the [AI lighting study](artwork/study/references/ai-mood.jpg) are kept as authoring references and are not loaded by the game.

The artwork combines AI-generated concept foundations with hand-edited pixel-art layers, animations, and mathematical chalkboard diagrams in Aseprite. It is part of this project and is not covered by the MIT software license.
